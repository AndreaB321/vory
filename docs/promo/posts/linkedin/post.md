Today I'm opening the public beta of Vory, the iPhone remote for a self‑hosted Hermes agent gateway.

The idea is simple. If you run your own AI agents, you shouldn't need a laptop open to work with them. Vory turns every agent on your gateway into a character you can talk to like a person in Messages, and it brings the one moment that actually needs you, the approval, to your pocket.

What I'm most proud of:

• Approvals where you are. When an agent wants to run a shell command or change a file, the card appears in the chat, as a notification, and in the Live Activity on the Lock Screen. Approve or deny without unlocking.

• Faces you can read. Thinking, using a tool, waiting for you, error, reconnecting. Each state is a held pose on the same piece of glass, so a glance tells you what every agent is doing.

• Nothing in between. Vory talks to a gateway you run, over your LAN, Tailscale or a Cloudflare Tunnel. No account, no middleman cloud. Chat summaries are generated on the phone.

It's a TestFlight public beta for iPhone on iOS 27. Everything in the video is real, drawn by the same code that draws the bots in the app.

If you run agents on your own hardware, I'd love your feedback. Details and the TestFlight link at vory.dev.
